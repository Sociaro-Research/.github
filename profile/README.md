# SOCIARO Research

**Applied research and engineering for generative AI.**

SOCIARO builds tools and infrastructure that help teams integrate, operate, and scale generative AI. Our roots are in performance marketing and creative production, where working at volume means solving practical problems: generation costs, fragmented provider APIs, reliable delivery, and visibility into usage.

[SOCIARO Gateway](https://sociaro.com) brings video, image, text, and audio models together through one API key, with consolidated billing, usage tracking, and support for teams building AI into their products and workflows.

## Our research

SOCIARO Research is our lab for exploring the technology behind these products. We study generative models and the systems needed to run them, with a particular interest in video generation and efficient inference.

Our interests include:

- **Generative media:** understanding model capabilities, limitations, and creative workflows.
- **Inference performance:** exploring ways to reduce generation time, memory requirements, and compute cost while evaluating quality tradeoffs.
- **Model deployment:** investigating how to run and serve generative workloads reliably.
- **Developer infrastructure:** making multiple models and providers easier to integrate into real products.

Our research is grounded in the challenges we encounter while building and using generative AI systems.

## Open source

### [OMG — Open Media Gateway](https://github.com/Sociaro-Research/open-media-gateway)

Our current public repository is **Open Media Gateway**, an open-source, self-hosted gateway for AI image, video, speech, and music generation.

OMG gives developers and teams a consistent API to submit generation jobs, track progress, and retrieve results across providers. It includes team access management, cost estimates and usage tracking, local or S3-compatible media storage, and a browser Playground with reusable prompt templates.

Connect your own provider accounts, run it on your infrastructure, and extend it with new provider integrations.

Released under the **Apache License 2.0**. Feedback, bug reports, and contributions are welcome.
